**Q1** (network with the second-largest connected component after removing a fraction $f$ of random nodes): Power-law network

**Q2** (Molloy-Reed criterion for a giant component): The average degree of a node in the largest connected component should be at least 2

**Q3** (optimal exponent $q$ for decentralized search on a 3-dimensional grid): **3**

**Q4** (TRUE about Kleinberg's model): In the two-dimensional Kleinberg model, a network generated with a large value of the exponent $q$ will not have small world properties

**Q5** (Kuramoto term quantifying coherence): The order parameter $R(t)$

**Q6** (TRUE about synchronization): In the Kuramoto model with two oscillators, they cannot synchronize if their angular frequency $\omega$ differs by more than $K$

**Q7** (attack critical threshold for a power-law network with $k_{min}=4$, $\gamma=4$): $0.3 < f_c < 0.65$
