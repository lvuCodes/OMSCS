**Q1** (in-degree power law with $\alpha > 2$):
1. False — The in-degree cumulative distribution function also follows a power law with the same exponent.

4. False — The out-degree distribution also follows a power-law distribution.

**Q2** (checking for a power-law distribution):
1. False — Find the degree range between $k_{sat}$ and $k_{cut}$ and check whether it decreases linearly in a log-linear scale plot.
2. True — The low degree saturation $k_{sat}$ causes a reduction, relative to an ideal power-law form, at low degrees.
3. True — The high degree cutoff $k_{cut}$ is generated by structural constraints and causes a deviation from an ideal power-law at very high degree values.
4. False — Rescale the distribution by $k - k_{sat}$, consider the range up to $k_{cut}$, and examine if the rescaled distribution decreases linearly in a log-log plot.
