# CS 8803 O20 — Summer 2025 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 8803 O20 2025-2.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Summer 2025). Instructors: Asif Khan, Colin Parker.

## Course Overview

Quantum computing promises exponential speedups for a class of important problems. The course emphasizes the hardware aspects of quantum computing, covering physical device operations using quantum-information mathematics rather than theoretical physics. Students gain foundational knowledge to work on quantum systems and hardware implementations.

## Prerequisites

Familiarity with college-level calculus, complex numbers, and linear algebra (vector spaces, inner product spaces, matrix manipulation, diagonalization, eigenvalues/vectors). Prior quantum-computing exposure helps but is not required; no physics background needed. Students need a computer capable of running Python and Qiskit.
