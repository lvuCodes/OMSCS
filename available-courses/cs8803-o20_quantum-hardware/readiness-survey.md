# CS 8803 O20 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-8803-o20-quantum-hardware](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-8803-o20-quantum-hardware)).

Familiarity with college-level calculus, complex numbers, and linear algebra (vector spaces, inner product spaces, matrix manipulation, diagonalization, eigenvalues/vectors). Prior quantum-computing exposure helps but is not required; no physics background needed.
