## Overview

The course develops understanding of the basic architectures, representations, and techniques for building knowledge-based AI agents and examines cognition-related issues. Coverage includes knowledge representation, planning, constraint satisfaction, case-based reasoning, and advanced topics such as analogical and visual reasoning. The course emphasizes learning through assignments and programming projects.
