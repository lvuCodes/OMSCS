# INTA 6450 — Summer 2024 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/INTA 6450 2024-2.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Summer 2024, section OCY/O01). Instructor: Jeff Borowitz. The Spring 2024 sample syllabus is similar and is not separately retained.

## Course Overview

The course examines big data foundations and analytics, covering computing technology and statistical principles, with a focus on the nature of underlying technical challenges and statistical assumptions in applied fields such as fraud detection and communication monitoring. It includes discussion of ethical implications and a data-analytics project using real datasets, allowing students to identify patterns and vulnerabilities.

## Prerequisites

No specific prerequisites required — many students with no programming or statistics background have found the class approachable. Students range from those with no technical experience to advanced PhD candidates.

## Grading

| Weight | Component |
|---|---|
| 30% | Lecture Activities — Discussions (11) and Quizzes (9) |
| 20% | Computing Activities — R Exercises (4) and a Crime-and-Punishment Python Exercise |
| 50% | Project Activities — Part 1 Proposal, Part 1 Peer Review, and Part 2 Paper |

Late work accepted within the due week for 50% credit; not accepted afterward. Tools: R with RStudio, and Python via hosted Jupyter notebooks. Consult the source PDF for the schedule and full policies.
