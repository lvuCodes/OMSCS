## Suggested Background (course page)

The OMSCS course page notes that the course aims to attract students from diverse backgrounds (math, physics, engineering, biology, neuroscience, sociology) and is kept as self-contained as possible. However, undergraduate-level knowledge of calculus, probability, linear algebra, and programming is necessary. If you can solve the prerequisite-test questions above — or do so after some revision of undergraduate probability and linear algebra — you are ready to start.
