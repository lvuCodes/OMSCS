- **Keith McGreggor** — Creator, Instructor

## Overview

The course provides real-world, hands-on learning on what it is like to create a startup. Students learn the language and values of entrepreneurship and then hone their entrepreneurial skills, focusing on practical startup experience rather than theoretical business planning. Students engage directly with customers, partners, and competitors to understand real startup dynamics, applying business models, customer-development processes, and agile methodologies to rapidly iterate products. Any startup topic of the student's choice is welcome. This elective does not count toward specializations but qualifies as a free elective.
