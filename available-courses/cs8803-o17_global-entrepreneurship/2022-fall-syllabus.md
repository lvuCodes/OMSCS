# CS 8803 O17 — Fall 2022 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 8803 O17 2022-3.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Fall 2022). Instructor: Keith McGreggor.

## Course Overview

The course provides real-world, hands-on learning on what it is like to create a startup. Students learn the language and values of entrepreneurship and then hone their entrepreneurial skills, focusing on practical startup experience rather than theoretical business planning. Students engage directly with customers, partners, and competitors to understand real startup dynamics, applying business models, customer-development processes, and agile methodologies to rapidly iterate products. Any startup topic of the student's choice is welcome. Counts as a free elective only.

## Grading

The course is individual-based and participation-driven. Graded components (per the weekly schedule) include lecture quizzes, weekly customer-discovery presentations (peer-reviewed), peer-feedback submissions, periodic surveys, and exams. Specific weights are administered through Canvas; consult the source PDF and Canvas for the exact distribution and due dates. First presentations are due the third week of class; assignments are due Sundays at 11:59 pm (GT main-campus timezone).
