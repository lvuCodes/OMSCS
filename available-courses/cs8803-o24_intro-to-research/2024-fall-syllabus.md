# CS 8803 O24 — Fall 2024 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 8803 O24 2024-3.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Fall 2024). Delivery: 100% web-based, asynchronous. Instructor: Nicholas Lytle.

## Course Overview

A general introduction to research methods and CS research more specifically. Students learn how to conduct, analyze, and communicate research in a variety of CS domains, completing a group literature review and an independent research proposal. The course is not foundational and does not count toward specializations but may serve as a free elective.

## Prerequisites

Familiarity with word processors and the ability to learn LaTeX (resources provided). Slack is required.
