## Overview

In Artificial Intelligence for Robotics, students learn how to program all the major systems of a robotic car, drawing on material from Sebastian Thrun (leader of Google and Stanford's autonomous driving team). The course teaches fundamental AI methods — probabilistic inference, planning, localization, tracking, mapping, and control — applied to autonomous vehicles, through six problem sets, multiple projects, a midterm, and a final exam.
