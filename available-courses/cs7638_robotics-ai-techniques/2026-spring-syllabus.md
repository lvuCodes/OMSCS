# CS 7638 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 7638 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026). Instructor: Jay Summet. Extensive programming in Python.

## Course Overview

In Artificial Intelligence for Robotics, students learn (from material created by Sebastian Thrun, leader of Google and Stanford's autonomous driving team) how to program the major systems of a robotic car. The course teaches fundamental AI methods — probabilistic inference, planning, localization, tracking, mapping, and control — applied to autonomous vehicles.

## Course Goals

Implement filters (histogram, Kalman, particle) for object localization; search algorithms (Dijkstra's, A*, value iteration) for path planning; PID control for autonomous course correction; path-smoothing algorithms; and SLAM for multi-dimensional movement.
