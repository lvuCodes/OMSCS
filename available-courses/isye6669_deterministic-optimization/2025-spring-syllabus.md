## Course Overview

The course teaches basic concepts, models, and algorithms in linear optimization, integer optimization, and convex optimization. It covers key optimization concepts, polyhedral theory, simplex and duality methods, nonlinear and convex conic optimization, and integer optimization with applications to data analytics. The course counts as a free elective but is not foundational.

## Prerequisites

Linear algebra, multivariate calculus, basic probability, and programming familiarity (Python preferred).
