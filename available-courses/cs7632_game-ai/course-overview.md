## Overview

The course explores artificial intelligence as applied to video game development, emphasizing gameplay experience over optimization — where general AI seeks a correct/optimal answer, Game AI aims to provide a fun gameplay experience and often involves strategic shortcuts due to computational constraints shared with graphics, sound, and physics. The curriculum addresses agent movement, path planning, decision making, goal-oriented behavior, learning, and procedural content generation.
