# CS 7632 — Spring 2025 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 7632 2025-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2025; cross-listed as CS 4731/CS 7632). Instructor: Jeff Wilson, PhD.

## Description

Artificial intelligence applied to video game development serves a different purpose than general AI research: where general AI seeks a correct or optimal answer, the goal of Game AI is to provide a fun gameplay experience — which may involve cutting corners, tricking the player, or "cheating" in implementation, under shared computational-resource constraints with graphics, sound, and physics. The course covers agent movement, path planning, decision making, goal-oriented behavior, learning, and procedural content generation.

## Technical Requirements

Most development uses the Unity Engine and C#; some assignments may use Python. Students need a Windows PC or Mac capable of running Unity.

## Suggested Background Knowledge

Familiarity with basic CS concepts (algorithms, data structures) and a strong software-development background. Unity is not assumed; familiarity with a strongly typed OO language such as Java is helpful.
