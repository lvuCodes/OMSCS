# CS 6675 — Summer 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 6675 2026-2.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Summer 2026). Instructor: Dr. Ling Liu.

Retained as the summer-term variant. The summer offering compresses all 26 modules into the shorter term; the course project is removed as a mandatory component and the grading weights are adjusted accordingly relative to the [Spring 2026 syllabus](2026-spring-syllabus.md) (the course objectives and topics are otherwise the same — see that file).

## Grading (Summer Variant)

| Weight | Component |
|---|---|
| 50% | Assignments (ten, 5% each) |
| 20% | Midterm test |
| 20% | Final-exam test |
| 10% | Participation |

All submissions are due 11:59pm Eastern on Sundays. Consult the source PDF for the full policy details.
