# CS 8803 O15 — Summer 2024 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 8803 O15 2024-2.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Summer 2024). Instructors: Laura Huffman, Esq.; Olufisayo Omojokun, PhD.

## Course Overview

The course helps computer scientists understand legal concepts relevant to their field. Topics include the US legal system, intellectual property, licensing and contracts, and data privacy. A distinctive component has students analyze commercial code as technical experts might during litigation. By completion, students should recognize fundamental legal issues affecting computing and determine when legal counsel is necessary. Counts as a free elective only.

## Prerequisites

Java programming experience and familiarity with integrated development environments (for the code-analysis work). Georgia Tech offers a free *Introduction to Object-Oriented Programming with Java* course for skill-building.
