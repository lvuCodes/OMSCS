## Overview

The course helps computer scientists understand legal concepts relevant to their field. Topics include the US legal system, intellectual property, licensing and contracts, and data privacy. A distinctive component involves students analyzing commercial code as technical experts might during litigation. By completion, students should recognize fundamental legal issues affecting computing and determine when legal counsel is necessary. Counts as a free elective only.
