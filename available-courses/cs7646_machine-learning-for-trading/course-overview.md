## Overview

This course introduces students to the real-world challenges of implementing machine-learning-based trading strategies, including the algorithmic steps from information gathering to market orders. It explores statistical methods including linear regression, Q-learning, KNN, and regression trees applied to stock trading, and comprises three mini-courses covering financial data manipulation, computational investing, and machine learning algorithms for trading.
