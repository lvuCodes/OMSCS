# CS 7646 — Fall 2022 Syllabus

Source: [Fall 2022 syllabus and schedule](https://lucylabs.gatech.edu/ml4t/fall2022/). Converted from the public course website (most recent public sample syllabus).

## Course Overview

Machine Learning for Trading. The course progresses from Python and statistics foundations through regression and ensemble learners, market mechanics and portfolio management, a market simulator, reinforcement (Q-)learning, and strategy evaluation.

## Required Materials

Referenced texts (select chapters): *Python for Finance*; *Probabilistic Machine Learning 1 & 2*; *Machine Learning*; *Introduction to Statistical Learning*; *What Hedge Funds Really Do*; *Foundations of Deep Reinforcement Learning* (Ch. 1).

## Late Policy

Deadlines Sundays 23:59 AoE. Penalty tiers: 0% by deadline; −10% up to 1 hour late; −25% up to 12 hours; −50% up to 24 hours; −100% beyond 24 hours. Emergency extensions via private Ed Discussion post.
