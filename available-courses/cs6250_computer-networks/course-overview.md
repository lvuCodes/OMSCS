## Overview

This course provides a quick refresh of introductory material, then offers broad coverage of protocols and algorithms spanning all layers of the Internet protocol stack. It covers Internet architecture evolution, the transport layer (TCP, congestion control), intradomain and interdomain routing, Software Defined Networking, network-security topics such as BGP hijacking, and multimedia applications and Content Delivery Networks. Hands-on projects address routing, SDN, BGP attacks, and Internet measurements.

CS 6250 is a foundational course and counts toward the Cybersecurity area.
