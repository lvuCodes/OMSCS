## Description

Overview of Internet architecture and its evolution, followed by broad coverage of protocols and algorithms across all layers of the Internet protocol stack (TCP and congestion control), intradomain/interdomain routing, peering, router design, Software Defined Networking, network-security topics such as BGP hijacking, and multimedia applications and Content Delivery Networks. Hands-on projects cover routing, SDN, BGP hijacking, and Internet measurements.

## Prerequisites

A prior networking course is not required, but the course is advanced; basic networking concepts are expected, and intermediate Python is required.

## Course Materials

No required textbook. Optional reference: *Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach* by Kurose & Ross, 7th edition. Content and logistics live in Canvas; discussion in Edstem.
