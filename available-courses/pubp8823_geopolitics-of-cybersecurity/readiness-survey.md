# PUBP 8823 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/pubp-8823-geopolitics-cybersecurity](https://omscs.gatech.edu/pubp-8823-geopolitics-cybersecurity)).

No formal prerequisites, though students should have preparation in either engineering or strategic cybersecurity foundations, including cryptography, security engineering, network security, human behavior, public policy, or international security.
