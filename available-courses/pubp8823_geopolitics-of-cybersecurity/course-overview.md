## Overview

The course teaches frameworks for analyzing power dynamics in cyberspace using international-relations concepts. Students examine how cyber power — organized deception via information systems for strategic advantage — differs from military, bargaining, and soft power. The course addresses coordination, warfare, coercion, and deception in cyber campaigns, serving both technology and political-science perspectives.
