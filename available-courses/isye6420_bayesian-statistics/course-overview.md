## Overview

An introduction to Bayesian statistical inference and its applications. By completion, students can select appropriate statistical models from real datasets, formulate prior information as probability distributions, and understand inference implications. The course emphasizes a hands-on approach with explicit results and centers on Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods.
