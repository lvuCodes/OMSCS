## Course Overview

An introduction to Bayesian statistical inference and its applications. Students learn to select appropriate statistical models from real datasets, formulate prior information as probability distributions, and understand inference implications. The course centers on Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods, covering theoretical concepts and step-by-step examples; deeper mathematical algorithm derivations and efficient chain setup are not the focus. Early homework uses one tool; from Homework #5 (after the midterm), students use Python.

## Prerequisites

Introductory probability and statistics; basic programming proficiency; comfort with calculus.
