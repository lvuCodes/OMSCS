# ISYE 6501 — Summer 2023 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/ISYE 6501 2023-2.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Summer 2023). Instructor: Joel Sokol. Uses R. The Spring 2023 sample syllabus is similar and is not separately retained.

## Course Overview

Analytical models are key to understanding data, generating predictions, and making business decisions. The course teaches how to select appropriate datasets, algorithms, and techniques for specific business problems using tools like R. Topics covered: classification, clustering, change detection, data smoothing, validation, prediction, optimization, experimentation, and decision making. The course is not foundational and counts as a free elective only.

## Prerequisites

Probability and statistics, basic programming proficiency, linear algebra, and basic calculus. An R background is helpful but optional.
