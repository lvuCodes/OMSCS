## Overview

Analytical models are key to understanding data, generating predictions, and making business decisions. The course teaches how to select appropriate datasets, algorithms, and techniques for specific business problems using tools like R. Topics include classification, clustering, change detection, data smoothing, validation, prediction, optimization, experimentation, and decision making. The course is not foundational and counts as a free elective only.
