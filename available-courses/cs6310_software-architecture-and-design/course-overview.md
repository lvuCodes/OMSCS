## Overview

Software Architecture and Design provides students with the principles and concepts involved in the analysis and design of large software systems. Learning objectives include understanding object-oriented design, developing software architectures, selecting architectural styles and design patterns, expressing specifications in UML, and discussing formal design languages with OCL.

CS 6310 is a foundational course counting toward the OMSCS degree requirements.
