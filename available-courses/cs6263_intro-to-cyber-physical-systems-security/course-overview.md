## Overview

An introduction to security within cyber-physical systems, including industrial control and critical infrastructure. The curriculum spans 14 modules: course introduction, CPS fundamentals, networking/security/control-theory foundations, industrial networks, threat history, ICS operations, network design/architecture/protocols, power delivery systems, ICS attacks and defenses, advanced concepts, privacy, and domain-specific threats such as transportation systems.
