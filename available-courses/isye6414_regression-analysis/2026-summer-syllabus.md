## Course Overview

The course introduces the principles and practice of regression analysis, covering linear regression, generalized linear models (GLMs), and model-selection techniques. Students engage in hands-on data-science work emphasizing active participation, independent exploration, and applying appropriate methods to real data — the complete workflow from exploratory data analysis through result communication, and regression's role in modern analytics and machine learning.

## Prerequisites

Familiarity with undergraduate or graduate-level statistics and probability, plus linear algebra; proficiency with Python and/or R. Jupyter notebooks, a PDF reader, and Git/GitHub knowledge are needed; an IDE (VS Code, PyCharm, RStudio) is optional.
