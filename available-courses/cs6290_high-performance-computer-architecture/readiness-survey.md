# CS 6290 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6290-high-performance-computer-architecture](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6290-high-performance-computer-architecture)).

Recommended prior experience: an undergraduate computer-organization course, knowledge of instruction sets and pipelining, and C/C++ and Linux familiarity. Self-assessment questions:

1. Have you taken a prior computer-organization course?
2. Are you comfortable with RISC instruction sets and assembly programming?
3. Are you familiar with pipelines and caches?
4. Are you proficient enough in C/C++ to modify large codebases (100K+ lines)?
5. Are you interested in processor simulation and performance analysis?
