## Course Overview

The course addresses concerns about algorithmic bias and the misuse of machine learning, examining how organizations deploy AI systems while considering fairness, ethics, and individual privacy rights. Students learn to design algorithms that handle large amounts of data while ensuring their outcomes are not misused.

## Modules

1. **Data, Individuals, and Society** — the power and impact of data on individuals and society
2. **The BS of Big Data** — underlying statistical fundamentals and challenges of big data
3. **Fairness in AI/ML** — understanding and applying basic AI/ML techniques with fairness considerations
4. **Bias Mitigation and Future Opportunities** — tools and methods to quantify and mitigate bias, considering ethical and legal issues
