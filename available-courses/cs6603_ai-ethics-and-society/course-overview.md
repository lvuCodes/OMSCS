## Overview

The course addresses concerns about algorithmic bias and misuse of machine learning. It examines how organizations deploy AI systems while considering fairness, ethics, and individual privacy rights, teaching students to design algorithms that effectively handle large amounts of data while ensuring their outcomes are not misused. Four modules structure the learning: data's societal impact; statistical fundamentals and big data challenges; fairness in ML systems; and practical bias-mitigation techniques.
