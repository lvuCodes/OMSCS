## Overview

Introduction to Health Informatics is designed so that students with a variety of backgrounds can gain basic proficiency in health informatics — the application of computing to healthcare delivery, population and public health, community-based clinical research, and the potential for big data and analytics to transform the field. Students engage through lab assignments and a practicum project using state-of-the-art health informatics tools and systems. Topics include health terminologies, FHIR, SMARTonFHIR, the OMOP Common Data Model, OHDSI Atlas, and AI/LLM applications.
