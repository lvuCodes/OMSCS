# CS 6440 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6440-intro-health-informatics](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6440-intro-health-informatics)).
