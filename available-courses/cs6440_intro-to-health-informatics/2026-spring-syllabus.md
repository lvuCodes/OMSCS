# CS 6440 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 6440 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026). Instructors: Jon Duke, Elizabeth Shivers.

## Description

CS 6440: Introduction to Health Informatics is designed so that students with a variety of backgrounds can gain basic proficiency in health informatics — the application of computing to healthcare delivery, population and public health, community-based clinical research, and the potential for big data and analytics to transform the field (distinct from bioinformatics). Students work through lab assignments and a practicum project with a TA mentor, gaining hands-on experience with modern tools and systems. Topics include health terminologies, the FHIR standard (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources), SMARTonFHIR, the OMOP Common Data Model and other OHDSI tooling, and AI/ML including LLM applications. Of six labs, four are in Python, with one each in JavaScript/TypeScript and Java; all are accessible with general coding knowledge.

## Course Learning Outcomes

1. General understanding of the technical side of healthcare (terminology systems, major data standards)
2. Understand and leverage the FHIR interoperability standard for healthcare data exchange
3. Understand and leverage the OMOP Common Data Model for research
4. Build secure healthcare applications and tools for clinical and/or research purposes using modern standards
5. Understand how to leverage healthcare data standards (FHIR, OMOP CDM) for AI/ML tasks

## Course Materials

No purchased materials are required. For projects, students must deploy their application on an accessible server (e.g., a cloud service); free providers/tiers are acceptable.

## Grading

Points-based (not percentage). **Grade scale:** A ≥ 90, B 80–90, C 70–80, D 60–70, F < 60. No rounding; not curved. Extra credit may allow grades to exceed 100. Coursework comprises lab assignments and a team practicum project. Consult the source PDF for full policies (attendance, academic integrity, and generative-AI usage restrictions).
