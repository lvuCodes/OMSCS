## Course Description

Introduction to computer vision: fundamentals of image formation, camera imaging geometry, feature detection and matching, stereo, motion estimation and tracking, image classification, and scene understanding. Applications include finding known models in images, depth recovery from stereo, camera calibration, image stabilization, automated alignment, tracking, and recognition. The focus is developing the intuitions and mathematics of the methods in lecture, then learning the difference between theory and practice in the problem sets.

## Late Policy

Three "free" late days across PS1–PS6 (counted in hours; 72 hours total). Late days do not apply to the policy quizzes, final project, or final exam (never accepted late). PS1–PS6 not accepted more than 2 weeks past due. Beyond the 3 free days, each extra late day incurs a 1% penalty off the final grade; a penalty applies per day even for a partial day. Late time is computed from the latest of code/report submission. Medical/family emergencies via the Dean of Students.
