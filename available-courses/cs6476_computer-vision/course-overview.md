## Overview

An introduction to computer vision including the fundamentals of image formation, camera imaging geometry, feature detection and matching, multiview geometry (stereo, motion estimation, and tracking), and classification. The course emphasizes both theoretical understanding and practical implementation, building intuition through mathematics and experimentation while highlighting the difference between theory and practice in the problem sets.
