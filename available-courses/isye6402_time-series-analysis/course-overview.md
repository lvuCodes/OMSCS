## Overview

Students explore standard time series modeling techniques including regression analysis, univariate ARMA/ARIMA modeling, (G)ARCH modeling, and Vector Autoregressive models with forecasting, identification, and diagnostics. The final module covers modern machine-learning forecasting systems such as Meta's Prophet, LinkedIn's Silverkite, and Uber's Orbit, plus a brief introduction to deep-learning approaches. A GitHub repository accompanies the course with data examples and implementations. The course is a free elective (non-foundational, no specialization credit).
