# CS 8803 O27 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-8803-o27-computer-graphics-ai-era](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-8803-o27-computer-graphics-ai-era)).

- Familiarity with computer graphics fundamentals at the level of CS 3451 (geometry, transformations, rendering, animation)
- Working understanding of calculus (differentiation, integration) and linear algebra
- No prior AI/ML background required; three preparatory lectures provided for students without graphics experience
