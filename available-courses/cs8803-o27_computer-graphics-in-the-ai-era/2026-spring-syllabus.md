# CS 8803 O27 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 8803 O27 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026). Instructor: Bo Zhu.

## Course Overview

A comprehensive introduction to modern computer graphics, focusing on techniques that have emerged over the past decade with a particular emphasis on AI-powered advancements in modeling, rendering, simulation, and animation. Key topics include Neural Signed Distance Fields, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), 3D Gaussian Splatting, differentiable neural physics, and generative models, exploring intersections across interactive graphical systems, visual content creation pipelines, and differentiable rendering frameworks. Advanced offering (not foundational); counts as a free elective only.

## Prerequisites

Familiarity with computer graphics fundamentals at the level of CS 3451 (geometry, transformations, rendering, animation); working understanding of calculus and linear algebra. No prior AI/ML background required (three preparatory lectures are provided for students without graphics experience).
