## Overview

A comprehensive introduction to modern computer graphics, focusing on techniques that have emerged over the past decade with a particular emphasis on AI-powered advancements in modeling, rendering, simulation, and animation. Key topics include Neural Signed Distance Fields, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), 3D Gaussian Splatting, differentiable neural physics, and generative models. The course explores intersections across interactive graphical systems, visual content creation pipelines, and differentiable rendering frameworks. This advanced offering (not foundational) can count as a free elective but does not apply toward specializations.
