# CS 7210 — Fall 2025 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 7210 2025-3.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Fall 2025). Delivery: 100% web-based, asynchronous, on Canvas. Instructor: Ada Gavrilovska. The Spring 2025 syllabus differs only in dates and is not separately retained.

## Course Overview

The course covers both fundamental concepts in distributed computing and the system designs enabling distributed applications. It provides in-depth understanding of core distributed-computing concepts, practical techniques for building system support, hands-on construction of distributed components through projects, and exposure to current state-of-the-art research.

## Prerequisites

No prerequisites are enforced, but a graduate-level understanding of computer systems and/or networking is expected (equivalent to CS 6200, CS 6210, or CS 6250).
