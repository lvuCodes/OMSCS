## Course Overview

The course examines the basic principles behind modern kinematic and physics-based animation techniques, focusing on mathematical foundations rather than animation software tools. Topics span keyframe animation, differential equations, particle dynamics, 3D orientation, rigid body simulation, collision/contact, character animation, inverse kinematics, motion capture, motion control, and reinforcement learning. Homework materials are distributed in Python.

## Prerequisites

A thorough understanding of linear algebra and vector calculus; computer graphics (CS 3451 recommended); object-oriented programming; Python experience encouraged.
