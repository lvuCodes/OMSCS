# CSE 6250 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CSE 6250 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026). Instructor: Jimeng Sun.

## Course Overview

The course examines big data analytics algorithms and systems within healthcare contexts, addressing challenges in analyzing large, complex medical datasets. Topics include predictive modeling, computational phenotyping, patient similarity analysis, scalable machine learning (online learning, similarity search), and big data systems (Hadoop, Spark, Graph DB).

## Prerequisites

CS 7641 (Machine Learning, emphasizing classification and clustering); proficiency in Scala, Python, and Java; data-handling and ETL experience (SQL, NoSQL); a minimum grade of C in MATH 3215/3225, ECE 3077, or ISYE 2027; and two of CX 4240, CS 4400, or CX 4242.
