## Overview

The course examines big data analytics algorithms and systems within healthcare contexts, addressing challenges in analyzing large, complex medical datasets. Topics include predictive modeling, computational phenotyping, patient similarity analysis, scalable machine learning (online learning, similarity search), and big data systems (Hadoop, Spark, Graph DB).
