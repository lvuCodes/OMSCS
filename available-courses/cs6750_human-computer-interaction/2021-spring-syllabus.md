# CS 6750 — Spring 2021 Syllabus

Source: [Spring 2021 syllabus and schedule](https://omscs6750.gatech.edu/spring-2021/). Converted from the public course website (most recent public sample syllabus).

## Course Overview

Human-Computer Interaction across five units: Introduction, Principles, Methods, Applications, and Conclusion — covering HCI fundamentals and practical design processes.

## Required Materials

Canvas (LMS), Peer Feedback system, PeerSurvey tool, course scripts and slides (via Google Drive), JDF format for written assignments.

## Grading

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Written Assignments (10 × 4%: 5 Principles, 5 Methods) | 40% |
| Tests (2 proctored exams × 15%) | 30% |
| Final Project (three check-ins) | 20% |
| Participation (peer reviews, forum, study participation) | 10% |

Scale: A (90+), B (80–90), C (70–80), D (60–70), F (<60). No curve.

## Late Policy

No late work accepted. Deadlines Sundays 23:59 AoE. Emergency exceptions via the Dean of Students office.

## Schedule

17-week semester with weekly assignments. Key dates: Test 1 (03/07/2021), final project due (04/25/2021), Test 2 (05/02/2021).
