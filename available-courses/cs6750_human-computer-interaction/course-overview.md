## Overview

An introductory course on human-computer interaction that does not presuppose earlier knowledge. The class addresses three main areas: interaction principles between humans and computers, user-centered design and evaluation techniques, and current HCI research including augmented reality, wearables, and robotics. Students analyze interfaces, conduct needfinding exercises, prototype user interfaces, evaluate designs with user feedback, and iterate on prototypes.
