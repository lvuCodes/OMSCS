## Overview

A holistic study of embedded systems focused on processor architectures, instruction sets, and compiler optimizations. The curriculum addresses the computational demands of modern embedded platforms — cellular devices, smartphones, tablets, and IoT systems — which require real-time performance, energy efficiency, compact code, and adaptable software stacks. Part I (Embedded Processor Architectures) covers instruction-level parallelism, ISA design, and micro-architectures including VLIW systems, scratch-pad memory, and branch prediction. Part II (Software Optimizations) examines compiler phases, register allocation, data layout, code/data compaction, and network-processor optimization.
