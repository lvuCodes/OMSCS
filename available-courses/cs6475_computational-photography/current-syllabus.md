# CS 6475 — Course Syllabus

Source: [Current syllabus](https://omscs6475.cc.gatech.edu/course-syllabus/) (evergreen course-website syllabus, not term-stamped). Converted from the public course website.

## Course Overview

Computational Photography covers digital imaging, cameras and optics, image analysis, applications such as panoramas and HDR, video processing, and computational cameras. The course spans 8 lessons with roughly 19 hours of lecture video.

## Required Materials / Software

- Ed (discussion), Canvas (LMS), Gradescope (submission), GitHub (assignment repository)
- LaTeX (report templates); Python (coding assignments)

## Grading

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| 5 Assignments (10% each) | 50% |
| 2 Projects (15% each) | 30% |
| Final Exam | 10% |
| 3 Notebook Quizzes | 8% |
| Plagiarism Quiz | 1% |
| Final Portfolio | 1% |

Scale: A (90+), B (80–89.99), C (70–79.99), D (60–69.99), F (<60).

## Late Policy

Assignments & projects: accepted up to 48 hours late with a flat 10% penalty per 24 hours (or fraction thereof); zero beyond 48 hours. No late submissions for quizzes, final portfolio, or final exam. All submissions due 12am AoE via Gradescope.

## Assignments & Projects

Five assignments (~1 week each, 10% each) and two projects (one mid-semester, one final; 2–3 weeks each). Specific due dates appear in the course schedule on the course website.
