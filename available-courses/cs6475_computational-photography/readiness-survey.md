# CS 6475 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6475-computational-photography](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6475-computational-photography)).

- Working knowledge of computer programming (preferably Python)
- College-level mathematics (matrices, vectors, differentiation, integration)
- Physics (vectors, optics) and probability (probability density functions)
- Basic camera operation knowledge
- Prior OpenCV and NumPy experience preferred
