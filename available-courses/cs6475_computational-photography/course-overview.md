## Overview

This class explores how computation impacts the entire workflow of photography, traditionally aimed at capturing light from a 3D scene to form a 2D image. The course emphasizes the scientific, perceptual, and artistic principles behind image-making, with a focus on the technical aspects of image capture and rendering using computational techniques.
