## Overview

The course teaches advanced techniques for writing exploits and patching vulnerabilities through hands-on laboratory work. It emphasizes Capture-The-Flag (CTF) problem-solving and covers reverse engineering, exploitation, binary analysis, and web security. The course is a free elective and does not count toward specializations.
