# CS 6265 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 6265 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026; titled "Information Security Lab: Reverse Engineering and Exploitation Labs"). Instructor: Taesoo Kim.

## Course Overview

The course teaches advanced techniques for writing exploits and patching vulnerabilities through an intense, hands-on security laboratory. A significant part involves solving Capture-The-Flag (CTF) challenges and discussing strategies, covering reverse engineering, exploitation, binary analysis, and web security. It is a free elective and does not count toward specializations.

## Prerequisites

Operating systems knowledge (equivalent to CS 3210 at Georgia Tech). Internet connection and Adobe Acrobat PDF reader.

## Grading

- **100% Lab.** No midterm or final exams.
- Failing to turn in a single full lab results in an F — students must submit at least one flag per lab (solving the tutorial counts).
- Expected distribution per group (undergraduate, masters, PhD): ~40% A, ~30–40% B, ~20–30% C and below.
  - A: five or more challenges per lab, plus all tutorials
  - B: four challenges per lab, plus all tutorials
  - C: up to three challenges per lab, plus all tutorials
- A one-week grace period applies (50% points after the due date).
