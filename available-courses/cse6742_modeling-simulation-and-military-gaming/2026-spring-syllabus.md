## Course Overview

The course focuses on the creation and application of computer simulations to model strategic international events concerning warfare. Students work in multidisciplinary teams (computing and international affairs students) using modeling tools, primarily NetLogo, to analyze historical and hypothetical military scenarios — for example, WWI's Schlieffen Plan, ancient battles (Gaugamela, Zama, Cannae), and modern conflicts (Gettysburg, Korean War). The course is project-based.
