## Overview

The course focuses on the creation and application of computer simulations to model strategic international events concerning warfare. Students work in multidisciplinary teams using modeling tools, primarily NetLogo, to analyze historical and hypothetical military scenarios. The course is project-based, requiring computing and international affairs students to work together to analyze specific questions using computer-based modeling and simulation tools.
