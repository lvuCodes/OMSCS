## Overview

Introduces cybersecurity from policy and management perspectives, organized around three organizational levels: individual organizations, national governments, and transnational entities. Four modules cover foundational concepts, organizational-level security policy, national public-policy frameworks, and international cyber-conflict dynamics. Students complete group projects, quizzes, discussions, and a final research paper. The course is mandatory for all Online MS in Cybersecurity tracks and counts as a free elective for other specializations.
