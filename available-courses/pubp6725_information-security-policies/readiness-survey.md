# PUBP 6725 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/pubp-6725-information-security-policies](https://omscs.gatech.edu/pubp-6725-information-security-policies)).

A basic understanding of computers and data networking, with exposure to technical material on protocols and vulnerabilities. Economics coursework and public-policy familiarity are beneficial; the National Research Council's *At the Nexus of Cybersecurity and Public Policy* provides helpful context.
