# PUBP 6725 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/PUBP 6725 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026). Delivery: 100% web-based, asynchronous, via Canvas. Instructors: Milton Mueller, Andreas Kuehn. The Summer 2025 sample syllabus is near-identical and is not separately retained.

## Course Overview

Introduces cybersecurity from policy and management perspectives, organized around three organizational levels: individual organizations, national governments, and transnational entities. Four modules cover foundational concepts, organizational-level security policy, national public-policy frameworks, and international cyber-conflict dynamics. Deliverables include small group projects, individually completed quizzes, discussions, and a final term paper. This is a required core course for all tracks in the Online MS in Cybersecurity and counts as a free elective for other specializations.

## Prerequisites

A basic understanding of computers and data networking, with exposure to technical material on protocols and vulnerabilities. Economics coursework and public-policy familiarity are beneficial.
