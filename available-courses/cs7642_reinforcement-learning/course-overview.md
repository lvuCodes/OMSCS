## Overview

The course explores automated decision-making from a computational perspective through a combination of classic papers and more recent work. Topics span Markov decision processes, stochastic and repeated games, partially observable MDPs, reinforcement learning, deep RL, and multi-agent deep RL. The course emphasizes generalization, exploration, and representation through lectures, readings, and practical projects progressing from discrete to continuous environments, culminating in self-driving robot training simulations.
