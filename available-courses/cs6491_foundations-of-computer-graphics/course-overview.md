## Overview

This course explores the mathematical, physical, and perceptual principles behind computer graphics, focusing on techniques for creating, representing, and displaying three-dimensional shapes and their properties. The curriculum divides into two main sections: Image Synthesis (first half, emphasizing ray-tracing principles and implementation) and 3D Modeling (second half). The course bridges foundational concepts with advanced topics suitable for graduate-level study.
