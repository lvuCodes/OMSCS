## Overview

This graduate-level introductory course teaches basic operating system abstractions, mechanisms, and their implementations. The curriculum emphasizes OS support for concurrency and synchronization, resource management across CPU, memory, and I/O, and distributed services. Practical components cover multithread programming, inter-process communication, and distributed interactions via RPC.

CS 6200 (formerly CS 8803 O02) is a foundational course counting toward the OMSCS degree requirements.
