## Schedule

The update schedule is on Google Drive, linked in Canvas under the Syllabus tab: [schedule spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1CoXvNt3Jbr3qT6JShfw9O5lZtKdtZpNlureo4i9u4zc/edit?usp=sharing).

Although this is an introductory course intended to supplement material that should have been mastered in undergrad, it is still intensive, with lectures, exams, and programming assignments.

## Assessment

The class has 3 programming assignments (Projects, 45%), a midterm (25%), and a final (25%), plus participation (5%).

### Projects (45%)

The programming assignments are named Project1, Project3, and Project4. All projects are independent (teams are not permitted, despite mentions in the course intro). Projects 1 and 3 are in C. Project 4 was recently updated in response to new developments in the Linux kernel and is in C++.

Instructions are provided for setting up a Linux-based environment. Do **not** download the VM linked in the Udacity Course Resources pages. For each project, relevant source code and object files are provided for local development and debugging, along with instructions for testing against the TA auto-grading frameworks. Ensure your `github.gatech.edu` account is properly set up to avoid access delays.

Tentative deadlines are listed in the schedule, along with suggested intermediate "milestone" deadlines (no official submission required) to avoid end-of-deadline overload.

### Exams (25% each)

The midterm and final are each worth 25%. Time intervals are listed in the schedule. Exams are proctored via **Honorlock** (the introductory lecture mentions ProctorU, which is no longer used). The midterm covers Parts I and II of the video lectures; the final covers Parts III and IV. The lecture schedule is a recommendation only — completion of lessons and practice quizzes is neither timed nor assessed.

### Participation (5%)

Earned through participation in the class online forums — mainly Piazza, though the TAs maintain a [Slack channel](https://omscs6200.slack.com/) many students find useful.

## Errata

Some videos still have issues (typos, or more significant problems such as a math error or image error), updated during the semester. Check for `#ERRATA` messages, and add any issues you discover to the pinned ERRATA thread in Piazza.

## Honor Code

Follow the [Georgia Tech Honor Code](https://policylibrary.gatech.edu/student-life/academic-honor-code). This applies to software too: do not use content (including software) found online without permission or proper credit. Additional information on Collaboration vs. Cheating is provided, and acknowledgment of the class policy is part of participation credit. Project repositories must be kept private.

## Communication

Weekly office hours are held via Zoom, tentatively Tuesdays 1pm EST starting the second week; exact time confirmed via a pinned `#office_hours` Piazza thread. Recordings are available afterward in the media gallery. All announcements are made via Piazza and pinned. The course Piazza site becomes enabled at the start of the semester (Aug. 18th). Ask general-interest questions as public Piazza posts; sensitive matters may use private posts visible to Instructors.
