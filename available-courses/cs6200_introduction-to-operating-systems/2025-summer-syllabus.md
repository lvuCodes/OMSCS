## Overview

This is a graduate-level introductory course in operating systems. It teaches the basic operating system abstractions, mechanisms, and their implementations. The core of the course focuses on OS support for concurrency (threads) and synchronization, resource management (CPU, memory, I/O), and distributed services. The practical component teaches multithread programming, inter-process communication, and distributed interactions via RPC.

This course is part of the Online MS in CS degree courses. The online content is available under Canvas → Modules.
