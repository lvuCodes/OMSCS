## Overview

The course explores how computing addresses challenges in developing regions and underserved communities. Students work in teams on semester-long projects with external partner organizations tackling social problems including homelessness, mental illness, autism, migrant farm worker health, childhood blindness, and food security. The course culminates in deployed solutions. CS 8903-C4G is a follow-up seminar offered each Fall where teams improve upon prior CS 6150 projects.
