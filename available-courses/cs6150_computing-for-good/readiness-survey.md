# CS 6150 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6150-computing-good](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6150-computing-good)).

Students should have completed graduate courses in at least two of: (A) Databases, (B) Networking, (C) Logistics, (D) Web development, (E) Global Health, (F) Technology and Society, (G) User Interface Design.
