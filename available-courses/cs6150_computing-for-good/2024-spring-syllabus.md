# CS 6150 — Spring 2024 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 6150 2024-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2024). Instructor: Santosh Vempala.

## Course Overview

The course explores how computing addresses challenges in developing regions and underserved communities. Students work in teams on semester-long projects with external partner organizations tackling social problems — homelessness, mental illness, autism, migrant farm worker health, childhood blindness, and food security — culminating in deployed solutions. CS 8903-C4G is a follow-up seminar offered each Fall where teams improve upon prior CS 6150 projects.

## Prerequisites

Students should have completed graduate courses in at least two of: databases, networking, logistics, web development, global health, technology and society, or user interface design.
