## Overview

CS 6601 is a survey of the field of Artificial Intelligence and is often taken as the first graduate course in the area. Students require familiarity with college-level mathematics (calculus, analytic geometry, linear algebra, probability) and computer science fundamentals (algorithms, big-O notation, data structures). A working knowledge of Python programming is essential, as it is the primary language for assignments. The course covers most of the required textbook, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (AIMA, 4th edition), a keystone of Georgia Tech's Intelligent Systems PhD qualifier exam.

The course qualifies as a foundational requirement and serves as core coursework for both the Computational Perception & Robotics and Artificial Intelligence specializations.
