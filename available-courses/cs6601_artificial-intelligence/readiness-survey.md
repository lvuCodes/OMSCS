# CS 6601 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6601-artificial-intelligence](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6601-artificial-intelligence)).

### Prerequisites & Readiness Assessment

Prospective students should honestly evaluate these questions:

1. Are you comfortable with Python programming, including IPython notebooks?
2. Have you completed multiple intensive, programming-focused courses?
3. Have you completed algorithms and data structures courses?
4. Can you commit approximately 9 weekly hours of study?
5. Are you proficient in linear algebra, probability, and calculus fundamentals?

If the answer to any of these is "No," the course may not be appropriate.
