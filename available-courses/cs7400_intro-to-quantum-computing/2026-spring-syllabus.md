# CS 7400 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 7400 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026). Instructor: Moin Qureshi.

## Course Overview

The course provides students in CS and ECE with fundamental background in quantum computing, equipping them with the computational aspects — physical devices, error-correction codes, system architecture, compilers, algorithms, and applications — for both near-term (NISQ) and long-term (fault-tolerant) quantum computing models. It includes a review of the linear algebra background required for the course.

## Course Goals

Develop familiarity with quantum gate operations and circuit construction; understand superposition and entanglement; analyze quantum algorithms (Deutsch–Jozsa, Bernstein–Vazirani, Grover, Shor); examine noise and error correction; and apply NISQ computation with qubit mapping and error mitigation.

## Grading

| Points | Component |
|---|---|
| 10 | Paper Reviews (five recent quantum-computing papers) |
| 10 | Knowledge Checks (best 10 of 12) |
| 20 | Problem Sets |
| 10 | Midterm Exam |
| 10 | Final Exam |

Total 100 points. Labs cover working on typical problems (e.g., evaluating Bernstein–Vazirani on a real IBM quantum computer, qubit allocation and routing, error mitigation). The midterm and final test the theory portion of the lectures. Consult the source PDF for the schedule and policies.
