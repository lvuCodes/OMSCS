## Overview

The course covers the computational aspects of quantum computing — physical devices, error-correction codes, system architecture, compilers, algorithms, and applications — emphasizing architecture, compiler design, and applications for both near-term (NISQ) and long-term (fault-tolerant) quantum computing models. Students develop familiarity with quantum gate operations and circuit construction, superposition and entanglement, quantum algorithms (Deutsch–Jozsa, Bernstein–Vazirani, Grover, Shor), noise and error correction, and NISQ computation with qubit mapping and error mitigation.
