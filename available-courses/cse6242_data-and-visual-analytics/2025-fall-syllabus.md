## Course Description

Introduces broad classes of techniques and tools for analyzing and visualizing data at scale, emphasizing how computation and visualization complement each other for effective analysis. Covers visual methods, computational techniques, and hybrid approaches. Students complete a significant team project exploring interactive data and visual analytics.

## Course Learning Outcomes

- Apply computational and visual techniques to real-world datasets
- Understand design principles of common visualization techniques
- Design and build interactive data visualizations using D3.js
- Gain hands-on experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP)
- Collaborate on substantial analytics projects and present goals, approach, novelty, and impact

## Prerequisites

No formal prerequisites for CSE 6242. Expected: proficiency in at least one high-level language (Python, C++, Java); debugging fluency; basic linear algebra, probability, and statistics; ability to learn multiple technologies quickly. OMS Analytics students should complete CSE 6040 first.

## Late Policy

48-hour grace period on all homework and project deliverables (no point deduction, marked late, no guaranteed staff support). Work incomplete by the end of the grace period receives zero. Medical/emergency exceptions via the Dean of Students; not retroactive.

## Homework

- **HW1:** Collecting & visualizing data, SQLite, D3 warmup, OpenRefine
- **HW2:** D3 graphs and visualization, Tableau
- **HW3:** Spark, Docker, Databricks, cloud services (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- **HW4:** PageRank, Random Forest, scikit-learn

## Minimum Computer Requirements

- 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended)
- 512 GB disk (SSD recommended)
- Dual-core Core i5 (8th gen+) or Mac M1+
