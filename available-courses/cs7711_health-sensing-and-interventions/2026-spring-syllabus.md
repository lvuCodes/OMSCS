## Course Overview

The course bridges the gap between the medical sciences and computing by exploring health measurement systems and interventions. Students investigate human physiology systems, health measurement devices, intervention technologies, medical device functionality, technology selection for specific problems, and custom device design. It accommodates both hardware-based solutions and software-as-a-medical-device (SaMD) approaches, including mobile applications and algorithmic solutions.

## Prerequisites

Familiarity with Python is beneficial but not required; the course has accommodated students with limited programming experience. Students need a smartphone and a computer with Python and Flutter programming capabilities.

## Grading

| Weight | Component |
|---|---|
| 35% | Assignments (2) |
| 25% | Participation — Quizzes (5%), Discussions (10%), Lab Reports (10%) |
| 40% | Projects — Proposal (5%), Mid-Term Presentation (5%), Final Presentation (15%), Final Report (15%) |

A standard 10-point scale is used for letter grades; a 10% penalty applies for late work. Students may begin project work only after providing IRB evidence. Consult the source PDF for the full policies.
