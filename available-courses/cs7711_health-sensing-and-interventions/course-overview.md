## Overview

The course bridges the gap between the medical sciences and computing by exploring health measurement systems and interventions. Students investigate human physiology systems, health measurement devices, intervention technologies, medical device functionality, technology selection for specific problems, and custom device design. It accommodates both hardware-based solutions and software-as-a-medical-device (SaMD) approaches, including mobile applications and algorithmic solutions.
