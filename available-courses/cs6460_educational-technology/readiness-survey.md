# CS 6460 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6460-educational-technology](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6460-educational-technology)).

Students should be able to answer "yes" to readiness questions including: completed the program's foundational requirement; comfortable with direct participation assessment; comfortable with substantial essay writing; comfortable working in groups or on individual projects; passionate about education/educational technology with project ideas; and comfortable with open-ended, student-driven learning.
