# CS 6460 — Summer 2020 Syllabus

Source: [Summer 2020 syllabus and schedule](https://omscs6460.gatech.edu/summer-2020/). Converted from the public course website (most recent public sample syllabus).

## Course Overview

Educational Technology, structured as an experiment where students complete projects in research, development, or content tracks, emphasizing genuine contribution to the course and the field.

## Required Materials

Canvas (LMS), Peer Feedback system, PeerSurvey tool, Slack (office hours), Research Guide & Course Library.

## Grading

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Written Assignments (3) | 15% |
| Qualifier Question | 10% |
| Project Proposal | 10% |
| Weekly Status Checks | 5% |
| Intermediate Milestones | 10% |
| Final Project | 20% |
| Final Paper | 10% |
| Final Presentation | 5% |
| Class Participation | 15% |

Scale: A (90+), B (80–90), C (70–80), D (60–70), F (<60). Assignments scored on an 11-point scale (0–10; 10 exceeds, 9 meets expectations); work below 9 may be resubmitted within a week, capped at 9.

## Late Policy

No late work accepted; emergency exceptions via the Dean of Students. Deadlines Sundays 23:59 AoE.
