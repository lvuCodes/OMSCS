## Overview

A heavily project-based class in which students deeply investigate the tools, methods, and theories behind educational technology; demonstrate mastery of a subset of the field; propose either implementing a tool for or conducting research in educational technology; and deliver a final project with a presentation and a publication-ready paper. The course operates without lectures, instead using a curated library of materials — academic publications, media articles, existing tools, past projects, and original interviews with experts in the field.
