## Course Overview

Digital Marketing has evolved from traditional marketing channels (radio, print, television) to include the online/internet channel. The course explores strategy, brand building, social media, lead generation, mobile marketing, and thought leadership through frameworks and real-world case studies. Not foundational; counts as a free elective only.

## Learning Outcomes

Explain major components of digital marketing strategy; understand implications of digital tactics for B2B/B2C objectives; identify components of each tactic in broader context; recognize digital marketing organizations, thought leaders, and channels; and differentiate disciplines/roles within the field.
