## Overview

Digital marketing has evolved from traditional channels (radio, print, television) to include the online/internet channel and has grown significantly over the past decade. The course explores strategy, brand building, social media, lead generation, mobile marketing, and thought leadership through frameworks and real-world case studies. Not foundational; counts as a free elective only.
