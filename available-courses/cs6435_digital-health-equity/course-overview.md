## Overview

Digital Health Equity introduces individual, interpersonal, and societal influences on health and how such influences create health disparities. The course examines how digital health technologies can be designed to achieve health equity and how various influences shape access to and impact of digital health systems. Students apply theory from the social, behavioral, and public health sciences to design and evaluate digital health systems for marginalized populations. The curriculum covers public health foundations, behavioral theories, social determinants of health, and human-centered design. Coursework includes response papers, design assignments, discussions, debates, and a semester-long team project addressing a health-equity issue with a software solution.
