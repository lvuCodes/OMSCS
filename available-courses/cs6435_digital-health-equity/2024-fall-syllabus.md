# CS 6435 — Fall 2024 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 6435 2024-3.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Fall 2024). Instructor: Andrea Parker.

## Course Description

Digital Health Equity introduces individual, interpersonal, and societal influences on health and how those influences create health disparities. The course examines how digital health technologies can be designed to achieve health equity, applying theory, empirical insights, and perspectives from the social, behavioral, and public health sciences to design and evaluate digital health systems that address disproportionate barriers to health for vulnerable and marginalized populations. Foundations of public health are examined alongside a central focus on applying human-centered design concepts to the digital-health-equity domain.

## Learning Outcomes

- Apply theory, empirical insights, and perspectives from the social, behavioral, and public health sciences to design and evaluate digital health systems
- Apply human-centered design concepts to design and critique digital health systems that address health disparities
