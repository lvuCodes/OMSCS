# ISYE 6644 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/ISYE 6644 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026). Instructor: Dave Goldsman. Uses R and Arena simulation software.

## Course Overview

The course covers modeling of discrete-event dynamic systems and introduces simulation-based methods for using these models to solve engineering design and analysis problems. The course is not foundational and counts as a free elective. Students learn to develop simulation models and conduct simulation studies, become familiar with simulation-language organization (extensive modeling using Arena), and review statistical aspects including input analysis, random-variate generation, output analysis, and variance-reduction techniques.

## Prerequisites

Basic calculus, probability, and statistics; bootcamps are provided for foundational material. Computer programming experience (e.g., Excel familiarity) is helpful.
