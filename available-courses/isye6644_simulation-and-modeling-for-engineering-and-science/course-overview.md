## Overview

The course covers modeling of discrete-event dynamic systems and introduces simulation-based methods for using these models to solve engineering design and analysis problems. Students learn to develop simulation models and conduct simulation studies, become familiar with simulation-language organization (extensive modeling using Arena), and review statistical aspects including input analysis, random-variate generation, output analysis, and variance reduction. The course is not foundational and counts as a free elective.
