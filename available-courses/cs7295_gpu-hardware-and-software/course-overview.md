## Overview

This course explores the software and hardware aspects of GPU development. Through hands-on projects, students gain basic CUDA programming skills, learn optimization techniques, and develop a solid understanding of GPU architecture. The course also covers compiler principles for GPU-related software issues and research papers on hardware challenges, aiming to deepen knowledge in compilers, programming, and computer architecture for modern GPUs.
