## Overview

Advanced Operating Systems is a graduate-level course that addresses a broad range of topics in operating system design and implementation, including: operating system structuring; synchronization, communication and scheduling in parallel systems; distributed systems, their communication mechanisms, distributed objects and middleware; failures and recovery management; and system support for Internet-scale computing.

CS 6210 is a foundational course counting toward the OMSCS degree requirements.
