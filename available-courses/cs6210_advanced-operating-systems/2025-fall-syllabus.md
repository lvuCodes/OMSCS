## Assessment Structure

Four programming projects (VM scheduling in KVM, barrier synchronization, distributed service using gRPC, MapReduce framework) and three tests (Test 1 over Lessons 1–4, Test 2 over Lessons 5–7, Test 3 / Final over Lessons 8–11).

## Reading List

The syllabus includes a per-lecture reading list of operating-systems research papers (e.g., Bershad et al., "Extensibility, Safety and Performance in the SPIN Operating System," *Proceedings of the 15th ACM Symposium on Operating Systems Principles*). Consult the source PDF for the complete paper list per lecture.
