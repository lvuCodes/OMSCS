# CS 6515 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6515-intro-graduate-algorithms](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6515-intro-graduate-algorithms)).

Students are expected to have an undergraduate course on the design and analysis of algorithms. This includes familiarity with basic graph algorithms (DFS, BFS, Dijkstra's algorithm), dynamic programming, divide-and-conquer approaches, recurrence solving, and asymptotic analysis. A discrete mathematics background is also assumed.

**Readiness option.** CS 8001 OLP is a one-credit seminar designed to fulfill the prerequisites for CS 6515.
