## Overview

This graduate-level algorithms course covers six major categories: Dynamic Programming, Divide and Conquer, Graph Algorithms, Max-Flow Algorithms, Linear Programming, and NP-Completeness (computational intractability). Students study fundamental algorithms in each domain and practice applying these techniques through design, analysis, and implementation.
