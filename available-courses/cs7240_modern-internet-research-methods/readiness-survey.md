# CS 7240 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-7240-modern-internet-research-methods](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-7240-modern-internet-research-methods)).

Prerequisites depend on the direction of the individual project. Coding is optional (e.g., for systematic literature reviews) but beneficial for systems, ML/AI, visualization, or algorithmic work. Students should be comfortable with open-ended problems and peer feedback.
