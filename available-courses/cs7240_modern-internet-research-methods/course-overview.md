## Overview

A research-oriented course covering new developments in Internet measurement techniques, with an emphasis on reliability, freedom, and security of modern Internet platforms. Students learn to describe current research in Internet measurements and cybersecurity (infrastructure hijacking, censorship, platform abuse, web trust, misinformation), apply passive and active measurement techniques to research questions, and develop a complete research project culminating in an academic paper and presentation.
