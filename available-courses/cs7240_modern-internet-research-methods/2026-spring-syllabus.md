# CS 7240 — Spring 2026 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 7240 2026-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2026; offered under the CS 8803 O23 shell). Instructor: Maria Konte. The Summer 2026 sample syllabus is near-identical and is not separately retained.

## Course Overview

A research-oriented course covering new developments in Internet measurement techniques, emphasizing reliability, freedom, and security of modern Internet platforms. Students describe current research in Internet measurements and cybersecurity (infrastructure hijacking, censorship, platform abuse, web trust, misinformation), learn passive and active measurement techniques, apply them to research questions, and develop a complete research project culminating in an academic paper and presentation. Topics: Measurement Techniques for Internet and Cybersecurity Analytics; Blocked Internet Access; Abuse on Online Platforms; and Sustainable Research & Ethics.

## Prerequisites

Prerequisites depend on the direction of the individual project. Coding is optional (e.g., for systematic literature reviews) but beneficial for systems, ML/AI, visualization, or algorithmic work. Students should be comfortable with open-ended problems and peer feedback.

## Grading

A project-driven scheme; the principal weighted components are:

| Weight | Component |
|---|---|
| 14% | Weekly Check-in Log & Meetings |
| 36% | Research Milestones (equally distributed) |
| 6% | Brainstorming assignments (I and II) |
| 5% | Research Problem and Related Work |
| 5% | Project Proposal |
| 5% | Lecture Quizzes |
| 4% | Final Project code |
| ~ | Smaller items — Research Interests questionnaire (1%), target-conference identification (1%), GT GitHub/Overleaf setup (1%), course-policy acknowledgment (0%) |
