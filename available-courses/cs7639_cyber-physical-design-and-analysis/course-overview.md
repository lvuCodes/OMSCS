## Overview

The course introduces tools and models for developing cyber-physical systems, which combine physical and software components. It covers model-based systems engineering and cyber-physical system verification and validation, with applications in aerospace, automotive, and medical devices. The curriculum addresses human-operator involvement and various formal verification methods.
