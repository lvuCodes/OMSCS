# CS 6260 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6260-applied-cryptography](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6260-applied-cryptography)).

No prior cryptography knowledge is needed. Requirements include:

- Mathematical maturity (reading and writing proofs)
- Discrete math competency
- Algorithms and complexity-theory fundamentals; big-O notation; proof by contradiction/contraposition
- Probability theory and modular-arithmetic basics
- Python familiarity for coding assignments

Elements of number theory are taught during the course. Students unfamiliar with permutations, sample spaces, random variables, or conditional probability should review an undergraduate discrete math text first.
