## Overview

A graduate-level introduction to modern cryptography focused on the classical goals of cryptography — data privacy, authenticity, and integrity. The course emphasizes practical application while maintaining theoretical rigor. Students learn cryptographic schemes (AES, CBC, RSA, DSA, TLS) with a focus on security definitions, evaluation methodologies, and parameter selection; internal block-cipher construction details are excluded.
