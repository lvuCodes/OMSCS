# MGT 8813 — Summer 2026 Syllabus

## Course Overview

The course prepares students to construct financial models and make business decisions through finance theory and analytical modeling. Two primary objectives: understanding basic time-value-of-money principles and finance theory (portfolio optimization, firm valuation); and developing Excel/modeling competencies (Excel functions and tools). Students learn to create cash-flow statements, apply time-value-of-money principles, build firm and equity valuation models, compute weighted average cost of capital, perform comparable-firm analysis, develop VBA models, construct Black–Scholes option-pricing models, and conduct stock portfolio analysis.

## Prerequisites

MGT 8803/6754 completion prior to enrollment is suggested. Microsoft Excel with the Analysis ToolPak and Solver (available to all Georgia Tech students).
