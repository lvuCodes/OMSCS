## Overview

The course prepares students to construct financial models and make business decisions through finance theory and analytical modeling. It emphasizes basic time-value-of-money principles and finance theory (portfolio optimization, firm valuation) and Excel/modeling competencies. Students learn to create cash-flow statements, build firm and equity valuation models, compute weighted average cost of capital, perform comparable-firm analysis, develop VBA models, construct Black–Scholes option-pricing models, and conduct stock portfolio analysis.
