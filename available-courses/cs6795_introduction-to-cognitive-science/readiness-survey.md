# CS 6795 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6795-introduction-cognitive-science](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6795-introduction-cognitive-science)).

No significant prerequisites. Students should be prepared to engage in scholarly discussion, read and analyze cognitive science literature, apply cognitive science perspectives to computing problems, use interactive modeling tools, and build and critique computational proof-of-concept systems.
