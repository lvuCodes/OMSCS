# CS 6795 — Fall 2025 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 6795 2025-3.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Fall 2025). A 3-credit graduate introductory course; fully online and asynchronous. Instructor: Keith McGreggor.

## Description

Cognitive science is an interdisciplinary study of mind and intelligence at the intersection of artificial intelligence, psychology, neurobiology, education, linguistics, anthropology, and philosophy. The course introduces foundational concepts, hypotheses, models, methods, issues, and debates in cognitive science, covering information-processing paradigms and their critiques, with applications to human-centered computing, information systems, digital media, educational technology, design, and human-robot interaction. The course is heavily project-based, including both guided and self-directed projects.

## Assessment Structure

Twelve quizzes (highest 11 of 12 counted), six small individual exercises (each producing a short report), and one self-directed semester-long term project unfolding over 12 weeks.

## Grading

| Weight | Component |
|---|---|
| 5% | Class surveys |
| 10% | Quizzes (Q1–Q12, best 11) |
| 35% | Individual Exercises (IE1–IE6) |
| 50% | Self-directed term project (milestones M1–M4) |

Quizzes are multiple-choice, based on the primary readings; two attempts allowed, highest recorded. Consult the source PDF for the readings list and full policies.
