## Overview

Cognitive science is an interdisciplinary study of mind and intelligence lying at the intersection of artificial intelligence, psychology, neurobiology, education, linguistics, anthropology, and philosophy. The course introduces foundational concepts, hypotheses, models, methods, issues, and debates in cognitive science. It covers information-processing paradigms and their critiques, with applications to human-centered computing, information systems, digital media, educational technology, design, and human-robot interaction.
