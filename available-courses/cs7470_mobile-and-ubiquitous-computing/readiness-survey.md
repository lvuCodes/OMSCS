# CS 7470 — Readiness / Suggested Background

Source: [course-overview.md](course-overview.md) → "Before Taking This Class" (from [https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-7470-mobile-ubiquitous-computing](https://omscs.gatech.edu/cs-7470-mobile-ubiquitous-computing)).

Undergraduate CS 2110 (minimum grade C) or CS 2261 (minimum grade C). Some projects benefit from web/version-control experience and data-analysis frameworks (Python, R, Matlab). Projects involving human participants require CITI certification.
