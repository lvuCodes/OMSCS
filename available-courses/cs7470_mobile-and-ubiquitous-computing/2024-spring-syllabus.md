# CS 7470 — Spring 2024 Syllabus

Source: `downloads/CS 7470 2024-1.pdf` (OMSCS sample syllabus, Spring 2024). Instructor: Thomas Ploetz.

## Course Overview

The course explores the third generation of computing, where humans continuously interact not with just one but many computing devices embedded into everyday environments. Students gain technical foundations in sensing, computing, and communication, then apply these concepts through practical projects addressing cutting-edge real-world problems and developing innovative solutions. The curriculum emphasizes both theoretical understanding and practical deployment of mobile and ubiquitous computing systems.

## Prerequisites

Undergraduate CS 2110 (minimum grade C) or CS 2261 (minimum grade C). Some projects benefit from experience with web platforms/version control and data-analysis frameworks (Python, R, or Matlab); others build on electrical-engineering foundations.

## Human-Subjects Research Requirement

Because projects may involve human participants, students must obtain valid CITI certification before working on their projects. Without valid CITI certification, students cannot fulfill the project-related assignment requirements.

## Grading

The course is heavily project-based, with team projects coordinated by the head TA and individually assessed responsibilities. One dedicated individual reading assignment ("The State of Ubicomp") — an individually written essay — is worth up to 10% of the overall grade. The remaining weight is distributed across project deliverables and assignments; all assignments are individual or team assignments assessed on subject-matter knowledge. Consult the source PDF and Canvas for the full assignment schedule and weighting.
