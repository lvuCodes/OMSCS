## Overview

The course explores the third generation of computing, where humans continuously interact not with just one but many computing devices embedded into everyday environments. Students gain technical foundations in sensing, computing, and communication, then apply these concepts through practical projects addressing real-world challenges. The curriculum emphasizes both theoretical understanding and practical deployment of mobile and ubiquitous computing systems.
