# 2026 Fall — Current OMSCS Courses

Listing of courses currently offered in Georgia Tech's Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMSCS). Most courses run in Fall and Spring; Summer offers a subset. [Course offering history (PDF)](https://omscs.gatech.edu/sites/default/files/documents/Other/Spring%202026%20OMSCS%20Course%20Offering%20History.pdf).

## Legend

- `*` — Foundational course (counts toward [Degree Requirements](https://omscs.gatech.edu/degree-requirements))
- `A` — Administered by OMS-Analytics (open to OMSCS students)
- `C` — Administered by OMS-Cybersecurity (open to OMSCS students)

Course name links to the official OMSCS course page. Description, Syllabi, and Resources are populated per course as each is processed.
